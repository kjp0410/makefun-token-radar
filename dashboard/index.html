Token Launch Radar

Every protocol that still records no token, ranked by what its launch would be worth under a value model fitted on the ones that already launched — with the steps to farm each one.

A fitted model, not a hand-set multiple

Launched protocols reveal what the market pays for TVL. This board fits a power law to all of them by least squares — log(market cap) = + × log(TVL) — plus one category offset per category, shrunk hard toward zero so small categories barely move the price. Every candidate term had to survive 5-fold cross-validation on the launched protocols themselves: the fitted curve beat the median-multiple model this board shipped first, while 7-day growth and token-less maturity adjustments raised held-out error and were dropped.

Each row's band runs from the 10th to the 90th percentile of the model's out-of-fold residuals — the honest spread of what launches actually priced at. A point value without its band would be false precision.
