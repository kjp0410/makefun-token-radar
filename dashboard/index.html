How the implied value is computed

Protocols that already launched reveal what the market pays per dollar of TVL. For each category this board takes the median market-cap / TVL multiple of that category's tokenised protocols in the snapshot (the global median where a category has fewer than three), then prices every still-tokenless protocol as TVL × peer multiple × growth factor × maturity factor. The growth factor is 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60% to +150%) / 250; the maturity factor is 0.9 + 0.1 × years tracked with no token, capped at four years. Tokenised protocols are the yardstick, never rows: the board is pre-token only, and a wrapped or staked product of a token that already trades is excluded by the same rule the snapshot's watchlist follows.
